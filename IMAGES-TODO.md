## 4. How to Drop In Real Photos

1. **Dimensions**:
   - Menu dish thumbnails: 600 × 450 px (4:3 ratio) or 800 × 800 px (1:1 ratio)
   - Gallery images: 1200 × 800 px (3:2 ratio) or 1920 × 1080 px (16:9 ratio)
   - Business card: Keep `img/card.jpg` at 16:9 ratio
2. **Format**: Save as `.jpg` or `.webp` compressed below 300 KB for optimal page load speed.
3. **Overwrite**: Simply place your new photos in `img/` with the corresponding filename, or update the `src="..."` attribute in the respective HTML files.
